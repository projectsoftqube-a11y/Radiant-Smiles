## 2. Role of the page

- **Quick-win page:** #11 today with an offer to promote. The hero states the method, the timeline and the offer in the first two sentences, so searchers see the answer and the price at once.
- **Honest expectations** (doesn't change dental work, deep stains may need veneers, sensitivity) reduce mismatched bookings and support trust.
- **Conversion:** offer repeated in hero, cost section and final CTA; one CTA per section.
- **Internal links:** up to the hub, across to veneers and cleanings, out to special offers and CareCredit.

## 3. AEO/GEO

Answer-first sentences written to be quoted:
- "Professional teeth whitening in Yardley, PA at Radiant Smiles @ Floral Vale uses custom-made trays and a whitening gel you wear at home for about 3 to 4 hours a night, for one to two weeks."
- "Your trays are ready in a day or two, and whitening is currently $100 off the regular $550 price."
- "Teeth whitening doesn't hurt for most people, but some notice tooth sensitivity while they're whitening." (ADA: usually temporary)
- "Crowns, veneers, fillings and bonding keep their original shade." (ADA)

**Checkable facts:** trays ready in 1-2 days; 3-4 hours nightly; 1-2 weeks; upper only or upper and lower; brown, yellow and spotted stains; $100 off / regular $550; CareCredit $200+ threshold.
**Entities:** Radiant Smiles @ Floral Vale, Yardley PA, American Dental Association, CareCredit.
**NAP:** name and Yardley, PA in the first sentence; full NAP in the final CTA.
**FAQ approach:** six questions, each opening with a direct answer ("No.", "Yes, when…", the price) in 42-57 words.

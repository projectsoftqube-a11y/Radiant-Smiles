## 3. AEO/GEO

Answer-first sentences written to be quoted:
- "Dental bonding repairs a chipped, cracked, discolored or slightly uneven tooth with tooth-colored resin that is sculpted, trimmed and polished in place." (MedicalProcedure description)
- "Dental bonding typically lasts three to five years before it needs repair."
- "Dental bonding and porcelain veneers both improve the look of front teeth. Bonding is quicker and more conservative; porcelain veneers last longer and resist stains better."

**Checkable facts:** four process steps (etch, bonding liquid, sculpt, trim/polish); often one visit; 3-5 years; resin can stain, chip or break.
**Entities:** Radiant Smiles @ Floral Vale, Yardley PA, dental bonding, porcelain veneers.
**NAP:** name and Yardley in the first two sentences; full NAP in the final CTA.
**FAQ approach:** five questions, 48-54 words, each opening with the answer.

## 2. Role of the page

- **Answers the price question directly.** The answer block and a four-row price table state the regular price, the offer and the free consultation and second opinion. Few local competitors publish a price, so this is a citable fact.
- **Kept in sync** with `/special-offers/` and the Invisalign page (handoff: one data source for all three).
- **Removes cost objections** in order: what changes the price, insurance, FSA, CareCredit, payment methods.
- Two CTAs inside the page plus the final CTA, all to a free consultation.

## 3. AEO/GEO

Answer-first sentences written to be quoted:
- "Invisalign at Radiant Smiles @ Floral Vale in Yardley, PA has a regular price of $5,800 and is currently $1,000 off, with a free consultation and second opinion." (first sentence)
- "The two biggest factors in the cost of Invisalign are how severe your alignment issues are and how long treatment takes." (site wording)
- "Some dental insurance plans cover part of Invisalign treatment through an orthodontic benefit."

**Checkable facts:** $5,800 regular; $1,000 off; free consultation and second opinion; FSA; CareCredit no interest if paid in full within six months on $200+; named PPO carriers from the accepted list.
**Entities:** Radiant Smiles @ Floral Vale, Yardley PA, Invisalign, CareCredit, named PPO carriers.
**FAQ approach:** five cost questions (47-55 words), each opening "Yes." or with the price.

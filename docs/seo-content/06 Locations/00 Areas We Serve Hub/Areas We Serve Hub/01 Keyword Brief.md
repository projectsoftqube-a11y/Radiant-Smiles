## Writing notes

Hub linking every location page. 'Bucks County' regional terms are NOT used: Amazing Smiles By Design's hub already owns 'dentist bucks county pa'.

- Unique content only: route from the town, local landmarks, town-specific FAQs. Never copy paragraphs between location pages.
- Verify every drive time and road in Google Maps before it goes into the copy.

## Content rules (all pages)

- NAP exactly as: Radiant Smiles @ Floral Vale, 117 Floral Vale Boulevard, Yardley, PA 19067, (215) 860-4600.
- No guaranteed outcomes, no prices unless the practice publishes them, no 'painless' promises.
- Only mention procedures, technology and financing partners the practice confirms it offers (see 00 Reference/00 Fact Sheet.md).
- Don't target keywords that belong to another page (see the Keyword Clusters spreadsheet in 00 Reference).
- Do not claim 24/7 or Sunday care. Do not say the practice is a periodontist, orthodontist or pediatric dentist.

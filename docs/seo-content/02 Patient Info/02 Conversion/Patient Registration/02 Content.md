## Getting Your Radiant Smiles Patient Forms

Registration forms: call us and we'll get them to you before your visit. Online registration isn't available yet, so please don't send your forms or health details through the website contact or appointment forms.

The forms ask for:

- Your details and contact information
- Your medical history and current medications
- Your dental history and the reason for your visit
- Your dental insurance details
- Parent or guardian details, for patients under 18
- Your consent and signature

---

## Medical History

Your medical history helps the dentist plan safe care. Please tell us about conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, an artificial heart valve or joint replacement, or rheumatic fever. List every medication you take, including heart medication, aspirin and blood thinners.

---

## Insurance Details

If you have dental insurance, have your card ready. You'll need the subscriber's name and date of birth, the insurance company and the member ID. We accept many PPO plans: see the [insurance plans we accept](/patient-information/insurance-payment-options/).

---

## Patients Under 18

A parent or guardian should complete the forms for patients under 18. All patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian at their visit.

---

## Keeping Your Health Information Private

Please don't send health information through our website contact or appointment forms. They aren't meant for private health details. If you have a question about your forms or your medical history, call (215) 860-4600 and we'll help.

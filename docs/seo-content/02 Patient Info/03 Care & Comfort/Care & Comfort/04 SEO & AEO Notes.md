## 2. Role of the page

Parent of Advanced Technology, Infection Control and Home Care Instructions, and the answer to the fear objection ("I'm nervous"). It uses only the comfort measures the site actually describes: clear explanations, prices before treatment, headphones and music, local anesthesia, "ask about sedation options", gentler technology (no impression trays, quieter electric hand-pieces, lasers with a light anesthetic spray) and published infection control.

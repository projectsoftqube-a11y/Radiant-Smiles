## Part B: Messaging

### Value proposition
Two Temple-trained dentists in one Yardley office who use dental microscopes and 3D imaging for precise, comfortable care, open Saturdays, with same-day emergency appointments and straightforward pricing for patients with or without insurance.

### Pillars and proof
1. **Precision:** dental microscopes, cone beam CT, iTero scans, digital X-rays; a patient's crowns sent back to the lab until they were right (Candice Coverdale review).
2. **Access:** Saturday hours (8–2), same-day emergency slots every business day, 30-minute emergency exam, about 15 minutes from Trenton.
3. **Affordability, plainly stated:** $89 new-patient visit (uninsured), $150/yr membership with 15% off treatment, 40+ PPO plans accepted, CareCredit, published offers on implants, Invisalign and whitening.

### Objections → honest answers
- "I don't have insurance" → $89 new-patient visit; $150/yr membership plan with 2 cleanings, exams, X-rays and 15% off treatment.
- "Is my plan accepted?" → we accept 40+ PPO plans; call to verify your specific plan.
- "Implants are too expensive" → $500 off (regular $3,500 for implant, abutment and crown), free consultation and second opinion, CareCredit.
- "I'm nervous" → headphones and music, ask about sedation options, explained before anything starts.
- "I work weekdays" → open Saturday 8–2.
- "I'm in New Jersey" → about 15 minutes from Trenton over the bridge; NJ PPO plans such as Horizon Blue Cross accepted.
- "I need to be seen today" → same-day emergency slots every business day.

### CTA lead-ins
- "Call (215) 860-4600 to book your visit."
- "Request an appointment online, or call and we'll find a time that works."
- "In pain? Call now and ask for a same-day emergency appointment."
- "Book your free implant consultation."

### Voice
Warm, plain and precise. Short sentences, real numbers, no hype. Never "best", "painless", "guaranteed".

Sample (warm, precise):
"Your first visit is about getting to know your mouth and your goals. We'll take digital X-rays, check your teeth and gums under magnification, and clean your teeth. Before anything else happens, we explain what we found and what it costs. No surprises, and no pressure to decide that day."

Sample (reassuring, emergency):
"A cracked tooth or a sudden toothache rarely waits for a convenient time. Call us first. We keep same-day emergency slots open every business day and on Saturday mornings, so you can usually be seen quickly for a focused exam, X-rays and a plan to stop the pain."

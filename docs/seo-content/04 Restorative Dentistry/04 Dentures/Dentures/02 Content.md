## Types of Dentures

The right denture depends on how many teeth you're missing, the health of any remaining teeth and how secure you want the denture to feel. Most dentures have a gum-colored acrylic base, with teeth made of plastic, porcelain or both.

### Full (conventional) dentures
A full denture replaces all the teeth in one jaw. A conventional denture is made after any remaining teeth are removed and the gums have healed. The upper denture covers the roof of your mouth with flesh-colored acrylic, while the lower one is horseshoe-shaped to leave room for your tongue.

### Partial dentures
A [partial denture](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/partial-dentures/) replaces one or more missing teeth when you still have healthy natural teeth. It fills the gaps and helps keep your remaining teeth from shifting.

### Immediate dentures
[Immediate dentures](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/immediate-dentures/) are made in advance and placed at the same appointment your remaining teeth are removed, so you don't go without teeth while you heal.

### Implant-retained dentures
[Implant-retained dentures](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/implant-retained-dentures/) attach to dental implants placed in the jaw. They move less than a conventional denture, and some types are fixed in place.

### Overdentures
A denture can also be made to fit over a few natural teeth that have had root canal treatment. Keeping those roots can help support the denture.

## Getting Dentures in Yardley, PA

Getting dentures starts with an exam, and your dentist will explain each step before you commit. The process usually looks like this:

1. **Exam and planning.** Your dentist checks your mouth, takes X-rays and reviews your health history, then explains which type of denture suits you and what it costs.
2. **Any extractions.** If teeth need to come out, they're removed first. With immediate dentures, your new teeth go in the same day.
3. **Impressions.** Impressions of your gums and any remaining teeth are used to make a denture shaped for your mouth.
4. **Fitting.** Your dentures are placed, and the fit and bite are checked and adjusted.
5. **Follow-up.** Sore spots are common while you get used to new dentures, and small adjustments usually fix them.

New dentures take some getting used to. Eating and speaking usually feel more natural with practice and a few adjustment visits.

## Denture Care: Keeping Your Dentures Clean and in Shape

Good denture care keeps your dentures clean, comfortable and free of damage. A few daily habits make the biggest difference.

- **Brush daily, inside and out.** Use a soft, large nylon denture brush with round-ended bristles.
- **Use denture cream, not toothpaste.** Toothpaste is too abrasive and can scratch the surface.
- **Rinse in cold water.** Dentures can warp if they're placed in hot water.
- **Keep them covered.** When you're not wearing your dentures, store them in water or a denture-cleaning solution so they don't dry out.
- **Look them over.** Check regularly for worn or chipped teeth.

If your dentures feel loose, have them checked right away. A loose denture can rub the gums and cause sore spots.

## Annual Denture Exams and Maintenance

Even with no natural teeth left, you should see the dentist once a year. An annual examination with Dr. Bhalala or Dr. Gadria helps make sure your denture is working properly and your mouth is healthy.

Your annual denture exam includes:

- An update of your medical and dental history
- An exam of your mouth, gums and the bone that supports your denture
- A check of your denture's stability and your bite
- An [oral cancer screening](/preventative-care/oral-cancer-screening/) to look for abnormal or pre-cancerous tissue
- A check for cracks, chips and broken or loose teeth on the denture
- Cleaning and polishing of your denture
- A review of your daily care routine

## Denture Relines and Repairs

Dentures need relining or replacing over time because the bone and gum ridges in your mouth slowly shrink. A denture that fit well at first can start to slip or rub.

- **Relines** reshape the inside of your denture to fit your gums again. A hard reline is usually recommended about every two years, and soft relines help tender gums.
- **Rebases** replace the whole pink acrylic base while keeping your existing denture teeth.
- **Repairs** fix a cracked or broken denture, often on the same day.

See [denture relines and repairs](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/denture-relines/) for the details on each.

## Denture FAQs

### How long do dentures last?
Dentures don't last forever, because the gums and bone under them slowly change shape. A hard reline is usually recommended about every two years to keep the fit snug, and worn or loose dentures eventually need replacing. Annual denture exams at Radiant Smiles @ Floral Vale help you know when it's time.

### How should I clean my dentures?
Brush your dentures inside and out every day with a soft, large nylon denture brush and denture cream rather than toothpaste, which is too abrasive. Rinse them in cold water, never hot, because heat can warp them. When they're out of your mouth, keep them covered in water or a denture-cleaning solution.

### Do I still need dental checkups if I wear dentures?
Yes. An annual exam checks the fit and bite of your denture, the health of your gums and the bone underneath, and includes an oral cancer screening. Your denture is also cleaned and polished, and checked for cracks, chips and loose teeth, so small problems are fixed early.

### Can I get dentures on the same day my teeth are removed?
Yes, with immediate dentures. Impressions are taken before your extractions, and the dentures are placed at the extraction appointment, so you leave with teeth. As your gums heal and shrink, the dentures need adjustments and later a permanent reline to fit well.

### What should I do if my denture breaks?
Call Radiant Smiles @ Floral Vale at (215) 860-4600. Cracked or broken dentures can often be repaired on the same day. Don't try to glue the denture yourself, because household glues can damage the material and change the fit. Bring all the broken pieces with you to the appointment.

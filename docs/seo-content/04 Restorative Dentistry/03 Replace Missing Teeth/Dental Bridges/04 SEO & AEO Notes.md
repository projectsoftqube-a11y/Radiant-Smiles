## 3. AEO/GEO

**Answer-first sentences written to be quoted:**

- "A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth with an artificial tooth held in place by the natural teeth on either side of the gap, called abutment teeth."
- "A bridge "bridges" the gap left by a missing tooth by attaching a replacement tooth to the healthy teeth next to it."
- "A bridge relies on the teeth beside the gap for support, while an implant replaces the root with a post in the jawbone and stands on its own."
- "A dental bridge usually takes two or three appointments."

**Answer block for the AI Overview:** the SERP shows an AI Overview that doesn't cite the practice. The first paragraph under the H1 is a self-contained 58-word answer (target 40-60) covering what it is, who it's for, the number of visits and where (Yardley, PA), so it can be lifted whole.

**Entities covered:** Radiant Smiles @ Floral Vale (name, address, phone); Yardley, PA; high-power dental microscopes; CareCredit; in-office membership plan; implant offer ($500 off, regular $3,500).

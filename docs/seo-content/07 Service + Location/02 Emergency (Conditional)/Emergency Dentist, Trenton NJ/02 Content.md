## What Counts as a Dental Emergency

A dental emergency is any problem that causes significant pain, damages a tooth or shows signs of infection. Call us if you have:

- **Toothache or dental pain**, especially pain that keeps you awake or gets worse when you bite
- **A broken, cracked or chipped tooth**
- **A knocked-out tooth:** keep it moist, preferably in milk or saliva, and call right away
- **A lost filling or crown**
- **An abscess or infected tooth**, such as swelling, a bad taste or a pimple-like bump on the gum

A toothache in Trenton, NJ that starts after our office has closed can be hard to judge. If the pain is severe or your face is swelling, go to a hospital emergency department. Otherwise, call us as soon as we open. [More about emergency dentistry](/emergency-dentistry/)

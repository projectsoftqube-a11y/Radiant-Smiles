Dr. Gadria was born and raised in California and now cares for patients at the practice's Floral Vale office, alongside Dr. Urvishkumar Bhalala. She treats each patient like family, which means taking the time to listen, explain and plan care around what matters to you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where did Dr. Gadria train?

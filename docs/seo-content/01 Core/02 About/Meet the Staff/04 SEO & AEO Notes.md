## 2. Role of the page

A short trust page that tells patients who they'll meet besides the dentists, and what hygienists, assistants and the front desk do for them. It is written to read complete **with the team grid hidden**, because no staff names exist yet. Once the practice supplies names and photos, the grid becomes the page's main content and a real E-E-A-T signal (named people, real roles).

## 3. AEO/GEO

**Answer-first sentence written to be quoted:** "The Radiant Smiles Yardley team is the group of hygienists, dental assistants and front-desk staff who work alongside Dr. Urvishkumar Bhalala and Dr. Jaspreet Gadria at 117 Floral Vale Boulevard."

**Entities covered:** the practice, both dentists, the three staff roles. Staff Person schema is held back until names are real.

**Role descriptions** are general (what a hygienist, assistant and front desk typically do) and tied only to services and payment options in the fact sheet. Nothing is claimed about individual staff (years of experience, certifications) because nothing is published.
